Approval is not the dangerous moment. Payment is. Horos re-checks everything an approval rested on, at the instant before money moves.

01 / mechanism

One gate, four hands

The model reads. Fixed rules decide. The executor re-checks the world. The network pays once. No single part can move money on its own.

Claude

Reads the email

As untrusted data, never as instructions. It must quote, word for word, the passage its verdict rests on.

Can add a hold. Never remove one.
Policy

Decides

The quote must be in the email. Bank-detail patterns hold on their own. Nothing in an email can set a payee or approve a payment.

Deterministic
Executor

Re-checks

At the start, after the passkey and just before confirming: the bill in T:0, the approval, the destination, the mandate's time left.

Three fresh reads
Visa · Airwallex

Pays once

A single-use Airi credential for the exact merchant, amount and approval. One bill, one payment, on any rail.
