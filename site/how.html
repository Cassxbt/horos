How it works

AI understands.Code decides.

Claude is the only part that reads language, and the only part that can never release money. Everything that lets a payment through is deterministic, journaled and re-checked at the moment of payment.

The one rule

A model can add a reason to hold. It can never remove one.

01 / the line

Where the model stops

Claude does

Read and cite

  • Classifies a supplier email: bank-detail change, routine, or unclear.
  • Quotes the exact passage, which must appear in the email to count.
  • Explains in one sentence for the AP lead.
  • Fails closed: unreachable or unparseable means held.
  • Earns its place: of 12 labelled emails, the 4 ambiguous ones were caught by its reading alone.
Code decides

Everything that moves money

  • Holds on bank-detail patterns whatever the model says: IBANs checked by their check digits.
  • Binds each approval to amount, bill version, destination and receiving account.
  • Re-reads T:0 before reserving and before confirming.
  • Reconciles only from the provider's own records.
02 / invariants

Rules the tests enforce

234 tests. Each fix was reverted until a named test failed.

  1. One bill, one paymentA single ledger reservation covers both rails. A second attempt is refused while the first is unresolved.
  2. Unknown is never paidA lost response, an unreadable provider or an unreachable model leaves the bill held, not reopened.
  3. Approvals expire on changeA new bill version, destination, verification or receiving account makes the old approval stale.
  4. Authority must outlast the callThe mandate and approval must have a minute left after the last read, or nothing is confirmed.
  5. Duplicates stay heldA copy of the invoice that was paid, voided or dropped keeps the other copy held.
  6. The books follow the providerAn accounting record is sent only when the provider settled exactly what was approved.
