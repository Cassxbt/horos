02 · The email arrives

“…and all future payments, to our new account”

Claude (claude-opus-5-5) reads it as untrusted data and must quote the passage it relied on, word for word. The quote is checked against the email before it counts.

The email asks us to send this Slack invoice and all future payments to a new Northbridge Commercial Bank IBAN, and to cancel any scheduled payment and resend it today. That pressure is a classic sign of payment fraud, so hold the payment until the change is confirmed with Slack using contact details we already have on file.
